# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Internship recruiters, freelance clients, and startup collaborators evaluating Nourelhak Maache as a high-potential software builder. They need to understand his technical range, initiative, communication skills, and ability to turn ambitious ideas into dependable web and AI-powered products.

## Product Purpose

Present Nourelhak as an ESI Algiers computer science student already operating with a product-builder mindset across full-stack engineering, backend systems, AI agents, automation, and data-heavy applications. Success means visitors quickly remember him, understand the value of projects such as Fidelio, and feel confident contacting him for an internship, collaboration, or freelance engagement.

## Brand Personality

Inventive, driven, precise. The voice is confident but grounded, technically credible without jargon-heavy posturing, and optimistic about building useful technology in Algeria and beyond.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic developer templates, résumé pages disguised as portfolios, anonymous neon cyberpunk styling, repetitive card grids, empty visual spectacle, and direct imitation of the reference portfolio. Motion and 3D must reveal personality and work rather than distract from them.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with a memorable human presence, then prove the engineering.
2. Show products as outcomes and systems, not lists of technologies.
3. Make ambition tangible through motion, depth, and decisive composition.
4. Balance emerging AI curiosity with demonstrated full-stack capability.
5. Give every audience a clear reason and easy path to start a conversation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA, maintain strong text contrast, full keyboard navigation, semantic structure, visible focus states, descriptive alternative text, and a complete reduced-motion experience. English is the default language. French is available throughout the portfolio and project case studies, with a persistent language preference. Light and dark themes are available.
